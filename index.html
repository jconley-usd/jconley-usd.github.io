03 — Research

Quantum circuits for clinical time series.

As a student at the University of San Diego, I research quantum machine learning. The current project applies a Quantum Neural Information Optimization Network (QNION) to clinical time series, including sepsis onset and heart rate predictions.

It uses variational quantum circuits benchmarked through qBraid. Training happens on a simulator. Inference runs on a quantum processor with the learned parameters held fixed. I presented the work as a conference poster with collaborators.

  1. 01 Simulator

    Train the variational circuit against the clinical series.

  2. 02 Hold

    Freeze the parameters once the model has settled.

  3. 03 QPU

    Run inference on the quantum processor.

  • Sepsis onset
  • Heart rate
  • Autonomic data
  • qBraid
